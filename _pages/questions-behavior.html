Guiding question 02 · Behavior

How does collaborating with AI shape the knowledge people build from data?

I study what people do when they make sense of data with AI: how they question, explain, challenge, accept and revise its contributions. The question I care most about is whether that collaboration produces real conceptual change, or whether people simply adopt what the AI says.

Research questions

  1. Interaction patterns Which kinds of interaction lead to new knowledge?
  2. Reliance and calibration When do people rely on AI appropriately, and when are they swayed by it? How do they notice and resolve contradictions between their reasoning and the AI's?
  3. Mental models How do people's mental models of AI shape their collaborative behavior, and how does collaboration change those models?
  4. Knowledge building How does sustained collaboration with AI change what people know, and how should it be designed for learning?

Dissertation research

Preprint · 2026

A Theoretical Model for Teacher Knowledge Development in AI-Supported Data Science Instruction

Emmanuel Adeloju

My dissertation follows two high school science teachers through a 12-hour professional development program in three phases: they learn to analyze data with an AI assistant, design lessons that use it, and teach those lessons. Using TPACK and reflective practice as lenses, it traces how their knowledge develops from one phase to the next. This paper sets out the model and study design.
