We conducted a comparative case study of two in-service high school science teachers to understand what patterns of roles and moves characterize their collaboration with LLM-based tools during data sensemaking tasks. Through interaction analysis and qualitative coding of 71 teacher-LLM turn pairs, our results indicate distinct roles assumed by the teacher and the tools, with the predominant pattern being teacher-judge and LLM-retriever role pairs. Moreover, the teacher-LLM collaboration was concentrated in the foraging than in the sensemaking loop. Study

A comparative case study of two high school science teachers (physics and biology) during four hours of data tasks with LLM-based tools. We coded 71 teacher–LLM turn pairs using four human–machine teaming role pairs (explorer–recommender, investigator–retriever, teacher–aligner, judge–supporter) and Pirolli and Card's foraging and sensemaking loops.
